# Ouroboros Vision — Historical Statement

Original publication: December 11, 2025. Clarified: November 20, 2026.

The original vision was to reuse heterogeneous and underutilized devices for AI computation, reducing dependence on centralized cloud infrastructure.

## Philosophy

- **Reuse over replace:** Evaluate available devices for useful work, subject to compatibility, safety and efficiency constraints. Not every device is suitable.
- **Distributed resources:** Allow computation outside centralized cloud services. This goal does not imply that orchestration itself must be decentralized; the historical architecture documents describe different proposals.
- **Trust as a primitive:** Treat reliability as something to evaluate, rather than assume. A validated trust mechanism is not demonstrated in the public excerpt.
- **Hardware agnosticism as a goal:** Seek portable execution across supported hardware and software stacks, without asserting universal compatibility or equal performance.

## Long-term aspiration

The original phrase "Linux of distributed AI inference" expressed an aspiration toward a neutral, extensible foundation. It was an analogy, not a claim of equivalent adoption, technical maturity, licensing or community governance. The public repository remains under its existing CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms; publication of the private implementation is not promised.
